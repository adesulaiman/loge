Walk me through how a new vendor gets approved.

A new vendor goes through three stages before it is activated:

01Submission. The vendor fills in the registration form and uploads supporting documents.
02Verification. The compliance team reviews each document and flags anything missing.
03Activation. Once approved, the vendor receives access and a welcome checklist.

If documents are incomplete, the request returns to submission with notes from the reviewer.

Sources 1onboarding.md 2checklist.md 3approval-flow.md
And what happens when documents are rejected?
When a reviewer rejects a document, the request goes back to the vendor with a short note explaining what to fix. The vendor can resubmit only the rejected files
