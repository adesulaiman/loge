Walk me through how a new vendor gets approved.

A new vendor goes through three stages:

01Submission. The vendor registers and uploads documents.
02Verification. Compliance reviews and flags gaps.
03Activation. Approved vendors get access and a checklist.
1Onboarding 2Checklist 3Approval
And what happens when documents are rejected?
The request goes back to the vendor with a short note on what to fix. Only the rejected files need to be resubmitted
